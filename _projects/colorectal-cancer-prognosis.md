title: Decoding Colorectal Cancer Prognosis
description: >-
  I investigated an 87-gene axis in colorectal cancer using clustering, survival
  analysis, and single-cell and spatial transcriptomics. I developed simulations
  to distinguish independent prognostic signals from effects of cellular
  composition. The score was associated with stromal remodeling, the CMS4 subtype,
  and worse survival in GEO and TCGA, although the survival association did not
  replicate in E-MTAB.
